Raspberry Pi 5 + 52Pi U2500 wall case

Assembly instructions for the v2 case. The ticks are not saved; reloading the page clears them.

This design has not been test-printed. Do the fit checks in section 2 before you put any electronics in.

What you need

Printed partspi5_case_base.stl, pi5_case_lid.stl, pi5_drill_template.stl
ElectronicsRaspberry Pi 5, 52Pi U2500 HAT with its standoffs, screws, ribbon and USB bridge, M.2 NVMe SSD (2230 or 2242)
Stack screws4 × M2.5 × 16 button head socket cap
Lid screws4 × M3 × 10 self-tapping (thread-forming for plastic). Do not go longer than 12 mm.
Wall screws2 × #6 or #8 pan head (or 4 mm). The head must be no wider than 9 mm and no thicker than 3 mm. Add anchors to suit your wall.
Tools1.5 mm hex key, screwdriver for the M3 and wall screws, drill, pencil, spirit level, 3 mm and 5 mm drill bits for cleaning holes

If something does not fit

ProblemChange in pi5_wall_case.py, then re-run it
Lid touches the Ethernet jacksRaise ABOVE_HAT
Lid lip too tight or too looseRaise or lower FIT (now 0.25)
Stack screws bottom out in the standoffsUse M2.5 × 14 screws and set SCREW_L to match. No reprint is needed.
Screw heads stand proud of the backRaise HEAD_DEPTH (now 1.8) or HEAD_D (now 5.0)
Ribbon is pinched at the closed endRaise GAP_X_TOP (now 6.0)
Wall screw head will not enter the keyholeRaise KEY_ENTRY_D (now 9.5) or KEY_HEAD_T (now 3.0)
